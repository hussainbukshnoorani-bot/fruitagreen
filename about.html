About Us

Fresh, fairly priced, and fairly sourced.

Our Story

FRUITA GREEN started with a simple idea: the fruits and vegetables on your table should taste like they were picked yesterday, because they were. We work directly with local farms to cut out the middlemen, the warehouses, and the days of transit that leave produce tired and flavorless.

What We Do

Every morning our team sources the day's freshest fruits and vegetables, checks them for quality, and gets them packed and on their way to you — usually within hours of harvest. Whether it's a quick grocery top-up or your weekly veggie box, we treat every order like it's going to our own kitchen.

Our Promise

Fresh, fairly priced, and fairly sourced. If something doesn't meet our standard, we'll make it right — no questions asked.
